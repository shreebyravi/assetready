# AssetReady: Launch plan

## Stages

| Stage | What | Who | Exit criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0. Validate | 8 to 10 interviews with FM handover managers and developer asset managers in Dubai and Abu Dhabi; trial two incumbent tools on the same sample package | Product | The problem is confirmed as frequent and costly by at least half; a gap remains after the incumbent trials |
| 1. Shadow pilot | One building. AssetReady runs on a copy of the real handover package; the FM team works as normal; findings compared afterwards | Product, one FM provider | Finds at least what the team found, with no unflagged false "ready"; quotes verifiable on real documents |
| 2. Assisted pilot | Same team uses AssetReady during a live handover; every decision still by a person | FM handover manager | Time per asset falls; no rise in issues found after acceptance |
| 3. Limited release | Two or three FM providers or developers | Product, customer success | Repeatable setup; rules configurable per client |

## Required before stage 1

- PDF and spreadsheet intake with page and cell references.
- A data processing agreement, UAE data residency where required, no training on client data.
- Repeated runs per asset, with disagreement flagged.
- A permanent audit trail outside the browser.

## Owners

| Area | Owner |
|---|---|
| Problem, scope, evals | Product manager |
| Rules per client | FM handover manager (client) |
| Life-safety policy | FM operations manager (client) |
| Data protection | Client's legal and IT, with our counsel |

## Post-launch metrics

- **North star:** share of assets with a reviewable, evidenced decision before the handover date.
- **Supporting:** time per asset; exceptions found before vs after acceptance; contractor turnaround on requests.
- **Guardrails:** zero acceptances by the tool; quote-check failure rate; tool error rate; how often repeated runs disagree.

## Rollback

Stop the agent, keep the rule checks and the exception list. The team falls back to its manual process with no loss, because the tool never changes records or sends messages.
