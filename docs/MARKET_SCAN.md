# AssetReady: Market scan

Checked 11 November 2026 from public pages only. Not finding a feature is not proof it doesn't exist.

| Category | Examples | What they do | Overlap with AssetReady |
|---|---|---|---|
| Handover and snagging | [PlanRadar](https://help.planradar.com/en/handovers-overview/) (handover forms, acceptance records, AI agents that act on project events), [Zutec](https://zutec.com/) (digital handover, O&M) | Collect handover documents, run inspections, track defects | High on collecting; unclear on cross-source reconciliation from the FM side |
| Asset data during construction | [Procore Asset Register](https://www.cimdata.com/en/industry-summary-articles/item/30573-procore-introduces-asset-register-within-asset-management-to-simplify-handover-from-contractor-to-owner), Autodesk Construction Cloud, [Zepth](https://meconstructionnews.com/58837/zepth-adds-ai-to-construction-project-management-platform/amp) (Dubai) | Capture and validate asset information as the building is built | High where the project uses them end to end |
| Structured data checks | COBie checkers such as [Morta](https://www.morta.io/solutions/use-cases/cobie-checker) | Missing fields, duplicates, naming rules on structured data | High for structured data; low for PDFs, remarks and emails |
| Owner platforms | [Kahua](https://resources.kahua.com/blog/ai-for-asset-centric-project-management) | Asset-centric project management, AI over project data | Medium |
| CAFM / CMMS | [JLL Corrigo](https://www.corrigo.com/), Maximo, Archibus | Run maintenance once assets are loaded | Low; AssetReady feeds them |

## Where AssetReady could fit

- **On the FM provider's side,** independent of the contractor's platform.
- **On mixed, messy inputs:** spreadsheets, certificates, warranties, remarks and correspondence from several companies.
- **As a decision aid before acceptance,** not a document store or a CAFM.

## What we couldn't check

- Whether PlanRadar's or Zutec's AI features already reconcile across sources (only public help pages read).
- Regional FM software and in-house tools at large UAE FM providers.
- Procore and Autodesk features behind sales demos.
- Whether FM providers would pay, or expect the developer to pay.

## Implication

The value has to be shown as a specific workflow (independent pre-acceptance check with cited evidence), not "AI that reads handover documents". The first validation step is to trial two incumbents on the same sample package.
